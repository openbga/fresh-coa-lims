From sample
to certificate.

One connected workflow for laboratory staff, reviewers and quality teams.

  1. Receive and sample

    Identify the lot, assign approved requirements and record sampling provenance.

  2. Test and investigate

    Record numeric observations, replicates and batch QC. Keep original results through corrections and out-of-specification investigations.

  3. Review and decide

    Independent reviewers sign their review. Quality checks completeness, results and holds before signing QC disposition.

  4. Issue and preserve

    Issue a versioned certificate of analysis. Later amendments add evidence while retaining the original certificate.

Built around
manufacturing QC.

Pharma and biotech. Supplements and food. Chemicals and medical devices.

Approved configuration sets the requirements for each segment within one system. Laboratory resources, analyst qualifications and stability records support the work alongside sampling and testing.

OpenBGA QLab is in development and uses synthetic data. Intended-use validation and operational acceptance remain deployment responsibilities. QC disposition is distinct from manufacturer batch release.
