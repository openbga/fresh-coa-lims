Rule sets

Presets are synthetic starting points. Approve a reviewed revision before use. Approved revisions cannot be edited.

A lot is received under one rule set. A rule set whose policy states applies_to is an additional one: it is never chosen at receipt and applies, with the lot's own rule set, to every lot whose requirements are assigned for a material, stage and scope it names.
