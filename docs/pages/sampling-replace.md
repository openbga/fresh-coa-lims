## Common tasks
### Review and sign the replacement
1. Check [[Versions in force now]], [[Requirements]] and [[Effect on the lot's tests]].
2. Enter [[Reason for the replacement]], [[Signing username]] and [[Signature PIN]].
3. Select [[Sign and replace requirements]].
4. If tests were superseded, assign the required tests again to repeat them under the new requirements.
### Choose between requirements stated differently
1. Under [[Requirements stated differently]], choose one statement for each test.
2. Select [[Review the replacement with these requirements]], then review and sign.
## Good to know
- A superseded test is kept as recorded. It takes no more work and never counts toward the disposition.
- The sampling plan is kept, because the lot's samples were taken under it.
- A supplier result proposed or accepted for the lot stops standing. It blocks the disposition until it is withdrawn on the lot record.
- You cannot sign if you recorded any of the lot's receipt, supplier certificate, sampling or test data, or proposed a supplier result for it.
- If the versions in force or the lot's tests change before you sign, nothing is signed and the page shows them as they are now.

## Purpose

Once testing has started, a quarantined lot keeps the requirements frozen for it. This page lets quality replace them, with a signature, by the versions in force now, for example after a specification, a method or the rule set was revised. Tests whose requirement changed are kept and marked superseded; the rest carry over. Where the specifications in force for the lot's scopes state a test differently, the signer also chooses here which requirement applies.

## Who can use it

- Sign-in required. **quality** role only; others receive 403 Forbidden. The **admin** role alone does not grant this page.
- Never a user who authored any of the lot's data: receipt details, supplier certificates, sampling records or test data, or who proposed a supplier's result for it (the same people who cannot sign its disposition). Such a user sees the page with a "Not permitted" alert, "You recorded receipt, supplier certificate, sampling or test data of this lot, or proposed a supplier's result for it. Someone who authored none of it replaces its requirements. Independence cannot be bypassed by any role, including administrators.", a **Back to <lot>** button and no form (status 403). No PIN is requested.
- The page opens only when a replacement can be considered. Otherwise you are returned to Lot requirements and sampling with one of these messages:
- "Only quarantined lots can have their requirements replaced."
- "Testing has not started on this lot. Assign its requirements again instead; that needs no signature before testing."
- "The lot has no assigned requirements to replace." (none assigned, or an applicability hold)
- "Requirement replacement is not configured for testing, so the lot keeps its requirements."
- "The versions frozen for this lot are the ones in force now. There is nothing to replace." (unless a requirement was chosen between specifications that state a test differently: the page then opens so that another one can be chosen, see Rules)

## Layout

- **Breadcrumbs**: Sampling, lot number, Replace requirements. Title "Replace requirements ·" and the lot number. Under it: lot number, "lot record revision N" and the status Quarantine.
- **Versions in force now**: an explanation that the replacement freezes the versions in force now "for the same material, stage and scopes, with the rule sets in force that name them", and keeps the sampling plan "because the lot's samples were taken under it". Facts: Material, stage and scope (or scopes, all of them listed); Rule set (the lot's version, then "→" and the version in force, or "· unchanged"); for a lot under one specification and its own rule set, Specification (the frozen version, then "→" and a link to the version in force, or "· unchanged"); for a lot under several scopes or with an additional rule set, **Specifications frozen for the lot** ("<code> vN (<scope>)", all of them) and one fact for every specification or additional rule set whose version differs, titled "Specification <code>" or "Rule set <code>": "<frozen> → <in force>", "<in force> · applies now" for one that did not apply before, or "<frozen> · no longer applies", or the single fact "Specifications and additional rule sets: Unchanged"; Sampling plan ("code vN · kept"). These other documents are listed only when the versions in force could be worked out: nothing refuses the replacement and every differing test has a choice.
- **Requirements stated differently** (count of tests; only when the specifications in force state a test differently): "Nothing is merged and neither statement is picked for you. A lot has to meet every specification that binds it: choose the statement under which a passing result also meets the others, or give in your reason why another specification does not bind this lot." Then either "Choose a requirement for each test, then review what the replacement would change." (a test still waits for a choice) or "The requirement selected for each test is the one the replacement applies; the others stay in the record. Your signature confirms the choice for the versions now in force." One group per test follows, headed by its code and name, with "The statements differ in: limits." and one option per statement: "**SPEC-TEST-EU v1** (eu-market) · Assay: 99 – 101 % · rounded to 2 decimals · method M-TEST-001 v2", naming every scope that states the test this way and ending "· information only" when not required, with the link **Open the specification** under it. For a test that has a choice, the statement chosen is listed first. Then the button **Review the replacement with these requirements**. A user who authored data of the lot sees the statements as a plain list, each starting with a link to the specification version, without choices and without the button.
- **Not signed** (alert in that section, after a signature was submitted while a test still waited for a choice): "Choose which requirement applies for each test the specifications state differently. Nothing was signed."
- **The requirements cannot be replaced now** (only when refused): a "Refused" alert listing every reason (see Rules), "Nothing was changed. The lot keeps the requirements it has." and **Back to <lot>**. No signing form is shown.
- **Requirements** table, one row per test line of the specification in force, then lines only the frozen requirements had: Test (code and name), Change ("Unchanged", "Changed", "Added" or "Removed"), Frozen for the lot, In force now. Each side reads like "98 – 102 % · rounded to 2 decimals · method M-TEST-001 v2", with "2 replicates" when more than one, the additions described under Statement wording, and "Information only" under a line that is not required. A missing side reads "Not in the frozen requirements" or "Not in the specification in force".
- **Effect on the lot's tests** (count of listed tests): an explanation that a test carries over only when its requirement is exactly the same (name, acceptance criterion, rounding, replicates and method version), that any other test is kept as recorded, marked superseded, takes no more work and never counts toward the disposition, and that assigning the required tests afterwards repeats a superseded test. Table: Test (link), Code, Sample, Status now ("Open", "Open · results or raw data recorded", "Awaiting review" or "Reviewed", with "· out of specification" when a result does not conform), Reportable, Effect ("Carries over", "Superseded no longer required" or "Superseded requirement changed"). Invalidated and already superseded tests are not listed.
- **Supplier results** (warning, when a supplier's result is proposed or accepted in place of a test of the lot): names each test code and acceptance, and says it "stops standing and blocks the disposition until it is withdrawn on the lot record. It can then be proposed again against the new requirements."
- While a test still waits for a choice, the Requirements table, the effect on the lot's tests and the signing form are not shown.
- **Sign replacement** (when nothing refuses it and every differing test has a choice): the statement of what the signature means (meaning **requirements replaced**, the lot, the record revision shown, how many tests are superseded and how many carry over, the resulting revision N + 1), "Signer:" with your name and username, a link "Enroll or change your signature PIN", the form, **Cancel** and **Sign and replace requirements**.
- **Foot line**: the lot number, when the page was viewed and by whom.

## Rules and hidden logic

- **What is resolved**: at the moment of signing, the approved version in force of the lot's own rule set (the highest version that applies now), the specification of every scope of the current requirements and their method versions in force for the same material and stage, and the additional rule sets in force that name the material, stage or scopes. An additional rule set approved since applies from the replacement; one that ended or no longer names the lot stops applying. The scopes cannot be changed here and the sampling plan version is kept. Nothing is merged or guessed.
- **Nothing to replace**: the page does not open ("The versions frozen for this lot are the ones in force now. There is nothing to replace.") when the lot's rule set version, every additional rule set and specification version and every requirement line are the same as frozen.
- **Choosing another requirement**: when nothing differs but the lot's requirements carry a choice between specifications that state a test differently, the page opens without a signing form. **Requirements stated differently** shows the choice frozen for the lot and reads "The requirement selected for each test is the one frozen for the lot, and the versions in force are the frozen ones: there is nothing to replace. To apply another requirement, choose it and review the replacement; the test made under the requirement it replaces is then superseded and repeated." Choosing another requirement and **Review the replacement with these requirements** shows the replacement that choice makes, which is signed like any other and is subject to every refusal above. A signature posted while nothing differs returns status 422 and records nothing.
- **Requirements stated differently**: specifications of the lot's scopes that state a test alike give one requirement. Where they differ, the scope named for the lot's current requirements (by a decision before testing or an earlier replacement) is carried only while that scope's specification still states the test exactly as it is frozen for the lot: same name, acceptance criterion, rounding, replicates and method version, and required alike. The carried choice is shown selected and can be changed. A choice whose statement was revised since is not carried; it is made again. A test without a carried choice needs one: choose, then **Review the replacement with these requirements** to see the effect before signing. Choosing the statement a test was made under carries that test over; choosing another supersedes it.
- **Who decided each choice**: the replacement keeps, for each test the specifications state differently, who decided it, when and why. When everything about the choice is as it was before the replacement (the scope that was named, the requirement that applies, every requirement it was chosen over and their specification versions), the record of whoever decided it earlier is kept (the signer of the decision before testing, or of an earlier replacement) and the choice counts as carried. Otherwise it is you, with the reason and the signing time of this replacement: you reviewed the statements as they are now. On Lot requirements and sampling the "Replaced" record then reads, for a carried choice, "Decided earlier by <name> and carried by this replacement: <that person's reason>".
- **Statement wording**: a statement adds "whole-number count" for a count result, "replicates judged by their mean only" when the replicates are not each held to the limits, and "up to 1 retest" (or "up to N retests") when retests are allowed; the Requirements table uses the same wording. Two statements of a test that would still read the same (they differ in something without visible effect, such as the replicate rule with a single replicate or the inclusion of a limit that is not set) each end with the differing values as approved, for example "· lower limit included: no" and "· lower limit included: yes". In the Requirements table a line can still be Changed with both sides reading the same when such a value is the only difference.
- **Signing without a choice**: a signature submitted while a test still waits for a choice returns status 422 with "Choose which requirement applies for each test the specifications state differently. Nothing was signed." The PIN is not checked and nothing is recorded. The server repeats the check under the locks ("Choose which requirement applies for each test the specifications state differently.") and accepts only a scope that states the test. A signature prepared with one choice does not sign another: see Stale page.
- **Refusals** (nothing is recorded and no hold is placed on the lot; each one listed):
- "Rule set <code> has no approved version effective now."
- the hold reasons of Assign requirements other than the sampling plan ones and the conflicts, for example "Specification <code> has no approved version effective now.", "Method <code> has no approved version effective now.", "Rule set requires tests missing from the specification: <codes>", "Rule set <code> v<N> requires tests that no specification applying to the lot requires: <codes>" or "No specification exists for <material> at stage <stage> and scope <scope>." (for example after an additional rule set that alone stood for the scope has ended)
- "Requirement for <code> is held: …" or "No required tests in the new requirements." when testing could not execute the new requirements
- "<investigation> is open on this lot (<trigger>). Conclude it first: a result is investigated against the requirement it was tested under."
- "<test> <code> holds results or raw-data files that were not submitted, and its requirement would change. Complete and submit it first, so that what was recorded is evaluated against the requirement it was tested under."
- **Carry over or supersede**: a test carries over when its stored requirement has the same content as the new one; the document it came from does not matter. A test of a code whose requirement changed, or that is no longer required (removed or information only), becomes Superseded. A superseded test and everything recorded on it are kept unchanged; it takes no more work, answers no requirement and never counts toward the disposition. Tests already invalidated or superseded are not touched.
- **After signing**: the lot gate reports a sample whose test was superseded ("Sample <ref> has no <code> test under the lot's current requirements: the earlier test was superseded when they were replaced. Assign the required tests."). **Assign required tests** on Lot testing then creates a linked repeat (kind "Repeat under replaced requirements") on the sample of the superseded test. A requirement line that was added gets an original test.
- **Supplier results**: every supplier's result proposed or accepted for the lot stops standing after a replacement, also for a requirement line that is unchanged, and blocks the disposition until it is withdrawn on the lot record.
- **Effect** (one transaction, or nothing): superseded tests change state and their revision increases by 1; a new requirement assignment (origin "Replaced during testing") supersedes the current one, under the same scopes, and keeps the evidence of what changed (also the other specification and additional rule set versions that changed, began to apply or stopped applying), each choice between differing statements with the scope that was named, what the statements differ in, the statements not applied and who decided it, when and why, the signing time, which tests carried over or were superseded, the supplier results and the lot revision the signature is bound to; the lot moves to the rule set version in force; the lot revision increases by 1 and the signature is bound to it. Earlier assignments and every test stay in the record.
- **Signature and audit**: signature meaning `requirements_replaced` on the lot (signer, server time, bound revision, content hash). Audit events with your reason: `signed.requirements_replaced` (lot), `lot.requirements_replaced` (new assignment: requirements, rule set and specification before and after, the other specification and additional rule set versions that changed, the scope chosen for each test stated differently, lines changed, tests superseded and carried over) and `test.superseded` for each superseded test (state before, why, result, requirement hash).
- **Independence**: the authorship check runs when the page opens and again when signing, under the locks ("You recorded receipt, supplier certificate, sampling or test data of this lot, …"). Holding several roles never waives it. Assigning requirements or tests is not authorship.
- **Stale page**: the server works out the replacement again when you sign. "The versions in force or the lot's tests changed after this page was opened. Review the replacement again before signing." when what you saw changed (a version approved or retired, a test assigned, submitted or reviewed, a supplier result proposed or withdrawn) or the choices submitted are not the ones the page was reviewed with; "Record changed. Reload and review before signing." when the lot revision changed; "The lot's tests changed after the replacement was prepared. Reload and review it again." when a test was added while signing. Any of the refusals above found at that moment is also reported.
- **After a refused signature**: the page that comes back (status 422) carries the fingerprint and the lot revision of what it shows, not those of the page that was refused. After a stale refusal it shows the replacement as it is now, with a "Not signed" alert under **Requirements stated differently** ("The versions in force or the lot's tests changed after this page was opened. Nothing was signed. The page shows them as they are now", ending "; choose again for the tests still listed without a choice" when there are any): a choice that was submitted is not kept, because the requirement chosen may be the one that changed, while a choice of the lot's frozen requirements still stands when its requirement is unchanged. After any other refusal the page shows the replacement for the choices submitted. Review it as shown and sign again; the PIN is entered again.
- **Identity and PIN**: "Enter your own signing username and PIN." on a mismatch (audited as `signature.identity_denied`); "Enroll a signature PIN first."; "Signature PIN was not accepted."; 5 consecutive failures lock the PIN for 15 minutes ("Signature PIN is temporarily locked."). Failures are recorded (`signature.denied`) even though the replacement is refused. "Session expired. Sign in again before signing." and "Sign in again before signing." for session problems.
- **Failed submission** returns status 422 with the form and the errors; the reason and username are kept, the PIN is not.
- **Not enforced by this page**: whether a change record exists for the replacement, and that a result meeting a chosen statement also meets the statements not chosen; the reason is free text.
