The package

A ZIP file with one folder, read from the database at one instant:

  • record.pdf: the printed copy. Every field of every record, each signature with the signer's name, the date and time and its meaning, and the audit trail with what each change replaced.
  • record.json: the electronic copy. Every stored field with its label and meaning, the signatures with what they signed, the audit trail and its reviews.
  • files: the raw-data files and the issued certificates of analysis, exactly as stored.
  • manifest.json and SHA256SUMS: the size and SHA-256 of every file, to check the copy with standard tools.

The stored checksums of signatures, files, certificates and requirements are recomputed. The first page of the printed copy says whether each one matched. Configuration the lot's records name, such as rule sets, specifications, suppliers and instruments, is included as it stands at the export.
