How to read this record

  • This is the printed copy. The electronic copy, record.json in the same package, holds every stored field with its label and meaning. manifest.json and SHA256SUMS give the size and SHA-256 of every file in the package.
  • Everything was read from the database at one instant, the export time above. Configuration the lot's records name is shown as it stood then.
  • Times are in UTC. Numbers are printed exactly as recorded. A dash means the field is blank.
  • A field naming another record shows its name and number (#). A field naming a person shows the name and username.
  • Each of the lot's records states whether the audit trail shows it being entered and how many events acted on it later. The Audit trail section lists every event: what each change replaced, or what an event recorded.
  • The checks recompute the SHA-256 of every file in the package, and the hash stored with each signature's content, certificate content, requirement assignment and approved specification, method and sampling plan version. Other hashes the records hold are copied, not recomputed.
  • Structured values longer than {{ print_json_limit }} characters, and values in the audit trail longer than {{ print_change_limit }} characters, are shortened here and kept in full in record.json.
